| Category | Adoption Rate | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| **Scheduling/dispatch software** | ~60% | Over 60% of contractors now rely on scheduling software |
| **Still using paper/spreadsheets** | ~40% | Paper tickets, text chains, whiteboard scheduling |

| **Use CRM** | Low (est. 20-30%) | Construction lags other industries significantly |

### Key Technology Pain Points

1. **Paper service tickets get lost.** Text chains get buried. Techs show up unprepared
2. **Billing inaccuracy** -- when info lives in separate spreadsheets and paper tickets, billing and payroll suffer
3. **No single source of truth** -- scheduling on whiteboard, dispatch via text, invoicing in spreadsheets
4. **Uncertain ROI** -- nearly two-thirds cite uncertain payback periods (often 24+ months) as the chief deterrent
5. **Cost and complexity** -- smaller firms find large-scale implementations risky

### Impact of Going Digital

- Companies using digital scheduling see **14-15% productivity gains** and **4-6% cost reductions**
- CRM users report up to **45% revenue increase**
- Construction management software market growing at **12.08% CAGR** (cloud solutions)

## 5. Referral vs. Paid Acquisition

### Where Jobs Come From

| Source | % of Jobs | Conversion Rate |
|--------|----------|-----------------|
| **Word-of-mouth / referrals** | #1 source (73-79% of homeowners use this) | 50%+ |
| **Google Search (organic + paid)** | 39-62% of homeowners search online | 12-31% (varies by channel) |
| **Angi / HomeAdvisor / Thumbtack** | ~34% of homeowners browse these | 10-15% (shared leads) |
| **Social media / other** | Remainder | Low single digits |

### Referral Economics

- Referred customers have **16% higher lifetime value** (conservative estimate)
- Some research shows **2-3x the lifetime value** of non-referred customers
- Referred customers are **$0.45/day more profitable** per Wharton Business School study
- CAC for referred customers is **$23.12 less** than non-referred
- Referral marketing generates **3-5x higher conversion rates** than paid ads
- Referral traffic has **2.5x lower CPA** than traditional advertising
- **69% of brands** say referral customers are more profitable
- Referred customers have **18% lower churn rate**

### Why Contractors Don't Have Formal Referral Programs

1. **Too busy doing the work** -- solo operators and small teams focus on execution, not marketing systems
2. **Don't know how** -- most contractors are tradespeople first, business operators second
3. **No technology** -- without CRM or automation, tracking referrals is manual and inconsistent
4. **"Word of mouth happens naturally" mindset** -- belief that good work speaks for itself without needing a system
5. **Fear of seeming pushy** -- reluctance to ask satisfied customers for referrals

## 8. What Contractors Actually Want

### What Would Make Them Switch / What They Need Most

1. **Exclusive leads** -- the single biggest ask. When a homeowner contacts you through LSAs, they contacted YOU, not you and 7 competitors. This is why contractors are migrating from Angi/Thumbtack to LSAs
2. **No long-term contracts** -- Angi's 12-month contracts with 30-35% cancellation penalties are universally hated
3. **Pay only for results** -- not for "interested" clicks on bad leads
4. **Transparent pricing** -- know what you're paying before you commit
5. **Verified lead quality** -- real projects, real budgets, real intent
6. **Control over lead volume** -- ability to pause/resume without penalties
7. **Trust/verification badges** -- Google Guaranteed badge increased CTR by **210%**

### Willingness to Pay

- Contractors are **actively shifting budget** from Angi/Thumbtack to Google LSAs for better lead quality and exclusivity
- The Google Guaranteed/Verified badge is so valuable that contractors pay the verification cost + per-lead fees willingly because conversion rates justify it
- Contractors who track numbers gravitate toward LSAs once they see the ROI difference
- Angi responded by reducing dependence on Google's network -- their proprietary channels now represent **80%+ of volume** because they know LSAs are eating their market share

### The Opportunity Gap

**What exists:**
- Shared lead marketplaces (Angi, Thumbtack) -- contractors hate them but use them because they don't know better
- Google LSAs -- better but limited and increasingly expensive
- DIY marketing (website, SEO, Google Ads) -- effective but requires expertise most contractors don't have

**What's missing:**
- A platform that combines **verified contractor profiles** (license, insurance, reviews) with **exclusive, high-intent leads**
- A system that turns the contractor's existing referral network into a **scalable, trackable asset**
- Technology that's **simple enough for a solo plumber** to use from a truck
- Transparent pricing with **no contracts, no cancellation penalties**
- A trust layer that replaces the "hope and pray" of finding a good contractor for homeowners

## Key Takeaways for Marketplace Strategy

### The Supply-Side Pain Is Real and Quantifiable

1. **$1,400+ effective cost per booked job on Angi** -- contractors are bleeding money on shared leads
2. **80% of trade businesses are solo operators** -- they need simple, mobile-first tools
3. **50% failure rate in 5 years** -- anything that helps them survive (steady leads, better margins) has massive value
4. **30% of leads are wasted** -- even the leads they DO get are poorly managed
5. **Referrals convert at 50%+ vs. 10-15% for shared leads** -- but contractors have no system to amplify referrals
6. **70% have no technology roadmap** -- the bar for "good enough" technology is very low
7. **550,000 plumber shortage by 2027** -- verified, available contractors will command premium positioning

### What Contractors Will Pay For

- Exclusive, verified leads: **$50-$150/lead** (if quality is proven)
- Trust/verification badges: Willingness demonstrated by Google Guaranteed adoption and 210% CTR lift
- Simple scheduling/dispatch: **$50-$200/month** (current FSM software pricing)
- CRM/lead management: **$30-$100/month** (if bundled with leads)

### Positioning Against Incumbents

| Incumbent Weakness | Marketplace Opportunity |
|-------------------|----------------------|
| Shared leads (3-8 contractors) | Exclusive matching |
| 12-month contracts | No contracts, cancel anytime |
| FTC complaints, lawsuits | Transparency-first model |
| No contractor verification | License + insurance + review verification |
| One-way value extraction | Two-sided value (contractors AND homeowners benefit) |
| Desktop-first, complex | Mobile-first, dead simple |
