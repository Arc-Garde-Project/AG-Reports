## 10. Home Services Industry Pain Points (Survey Data)

### Trust Gap
| Finding | Stat | Source |
|---------|------|--------|
| Homeowners worried about unreliable contractors | 70% | [Leaf Home Survey, Jan 2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/leaf-home-survey-reveals-trust-gap-in-home-improvement-industry-302361103.html) |
| Homeowners report being deceived by providers | 41% | [Leaf Home Survey](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/leaf-home-survey-reveals-trust-gap-in-home-improvement-industry-302361103.html) |
| Homeowners with little/no trust in providers | 16% | [Roto-Rooter Survey, Apr 2024](https://www.rotorooter.com/blog/plumbing/state-of-home-repair-in-2024/) |
| Homeowners "somewhat" trust providers | 50%+ | [Roto-Rooter Survey](https://www.rotorooter.com/blog/plumbing/state-of-home-repair-in-2024/) |
| Had negative experience from third-party platform | 30% | [Housecall Pro 2025](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/home-service-customer-service-report-trends-statistics/) |

### Top Homeowner Frustrations (Housecall Pro 2025 Survey)
1. Late arrivals
2. Unprofessional technicians
3. Unclear/non-transparent pricing
4. Lack of status updates during the job
5. Incomplete jobs

### What Homeowners Actually Prioritize (BBB Survey)
| Priority | % of Consumers |
|----------|---------------|
| Trust and reputation | 55% |
| Quality of work | 28% |
| Online reviews and ratings | 11% |
| Price and affordability | 6% |

Source: [BBB Survey via WREX](https://www.wrex.com/news/business/trust-not-price-tops-consumers-home-improvement-choices-says-bbb-survey/article_99d471de-05ae-474d-8099-0f64480b59d3.html)

### Service Quality Gap
- ~20% of homeowners cannot remember the last time a contractor provided exceptional service ([Housecall Pro](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/home-service-customer-service-report-trends-statistics/))
- 63% of residential services businesses report consistent growth, BUT 19% are just "surviving" and 18% are "struggling" ([ServiceTitan Research, Mar 2025](https://www.servicetitan.com/press/residential-industry-report-2025))

### What Homeowners Want (Emerging Expectations)
- Online scheduling and instant booking
- Transparent, upfront pricing before the appointment
- Real-time job status updates
- Verified contractor quality/credentials
- Seamless digital experience (quote-to-payment)
