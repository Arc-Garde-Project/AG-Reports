Key Insights

Month 2 delivered 77,619 total views across 6 active platforms. That is a +73.6% jump from February (44,698). Every metric is trending up.

YouTube is the powerhouse at 29,513 views and 43.1 watch hours. Views up 54% from Feb (19,207). The algorithm is pushing content to new audiences hard.

Instagram had a breakout month at 19,097 views (up 177% from 6,907). Follower growth hit +27, the strongest single-platform gain. 443 interactions shows real engagement.

Facebook quietly tripled from ~9,500 impressions to 12,700 views with 99 interactions and 8,500 unique viewers. Reliable distribution channel.

TikTok grew views from 8,540 to 15,000 (+75%) with 274 likes. However, lost 5 followers (12 to 7). Views are up but retention is not converting yet.

X/Twitter shows zero traction. 861 impressions, 0 likes, 0 reposts across the month. 4.22% engagement rate is from replies only. Lowest ROI platform.

Total follower count reached 60 across all platforms (up from 38). Instagram and LinkedIn are the only platforms adding followers. YouTube and TikTok both lost subscribers despite strong view growth.

Website Insights

288 unique visitors (166 desktop, 133 mobile) in month 2 with zero paid traffic. All organic from social channels.

82.5% bounce rate is high. Most visitors land on Home and leave without exploring. Only 16.6% make it to Pricing, only 5.1% reach Book.

4m 47s average session is strong for a services site. People who stay are engaged.

Facebook is the #1 traffic source (25 combined from facebook.com + m.facebook.com). Instagram is #2 (14 combined from instagram.com + l.instagram.com). Social is doing the heavy lifting.

Only 5 visitors from Google. Organic search is not a channel yet. SEO opportunity if content marketing ramps up.

The funnel drops hard after Home. 253 Home → 42 Pricing → 13 Book = 5.1% conversion to booking page. The Home page needs a stronger CTA path to Pricing/Book.
