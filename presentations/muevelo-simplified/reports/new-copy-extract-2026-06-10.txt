SLIDE 2EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Muevelo West represents the acquisition and transition of Chico's Montebello, an established nightlife venue with a proven operating history, into a more intentional and actively managed entertainment concept operated under the Muevelo brand.
Unlike a traditional startup venue, Muevelo West benefits from an existing customer base, established market presence, active licenses, operational infrastructure, and approximately fourteen years of remaining lease control.
The opportunity consists of a $600,000 acquisition and working capital requirement supported by a separate $130,000 seller carry note.
Management's objective is to build a sustainable entertainment business focused on operational excellence, customer experience, strategic event programming, marketing execution, and long-term enterprise value.

SLIDE 6UNDERSTANDING THE REVENUE DECLINE
Management believes the decline in revenue during 2025 was influenced by:
• Immigration enforcement concerns throughout Southern California
• Reduced attendance within portions of the Hispanic community
• Reduction in operating schedule from approximately four nights per week to approximately two nights per week
• Reduced marketing efforts
• Ownership gradually winding down operations prior to transition
Historical Chico's results should be viewed as evidence of venue viability rather than a projection of future Muevelo performance.

SLIDE 7THE MUEVELO DIFFERENCE
Historical Chico's performance supports acquisition value but does not reflect the intended Muevelo operating model.
Management intends to implement:
• Active ownership involvement
• Consistent marketing campaigns
• Strategic event programming
• Influencer partnerships
• Community engagement initiatives
• Enhanced customer experience
• Revenue diversification initiatives
The objective is to maximize the potential of the venue through more intentional execution.

SLIDE 8TRANSITION STRATEGY & CONTINUITY PLAN
As part of the acquisition:
• Existing ownership is expected to remain involved for approximately three months following closing
• Existing vendor relationships will be transferred
• Existing operational knowledge will be shared
• Existing customer awareness will be preserved
• Existing staff relationships will be maintained where appropriate
This transition period is intended to reduce operational risk and support continuity.
