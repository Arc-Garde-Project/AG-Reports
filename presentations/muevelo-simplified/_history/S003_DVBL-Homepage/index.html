Muevelo West at Chico's Montebello

Founding Investor Circle Opportunity

Executive Summary

An Established Venue, Run With Intention

Muevelo West represents the acquisition and transition of Chico's Montebello, an established nightlife venue with a proven operating history, into a more intentional and actively managed entertainment concept operated under the Muevelo brand.

Unlike a traditional startup venue, Muevelo West benefits from an established market presence, existing customer awareness, active licenses, venue infrastructure, and long-term lease control.

The opportunity consists of a 600,000 dollar capital raise and a separate 130,000 dollar seller carry note designed to support the transition and align ownership interests.

Management's objective is to create a long-term entertainment brand focused on operational consistency, customer experience, event programming, marketing execution, and sustainable profitability.

Why This Opportunity Exists

Most Nightlife Starts From Zero. This Does Not.

Most nightlife concepts begin with significant startup risk. Muevelo West begins with:

  • Existing venue infrastructure
  • Existing customer recognition
  • Existing liquor licenses
  • Existing operating history
  • Existing market awareness
  • Existing vendor relationships
  • Existing employee knowledge

This allows management to focus on growth and execution rather than building an operation from the ground up.

Understanding the Revenue Decline

Context Behind 2025

Management believes the decline in revenue during 2025 was influenced by several factors:

  • Increased concern surrounding immigration enforcement activity throughout Southern California
  • Reduced attendance within portions of the Hispanic community
  • Reduction in operating schedule from approximately 4 nights per week to approximately 2 nights per week
  • Reduced marketing activity
  • Ownership gradually winding down operations before transition

Historical performance should be viewed as evidence of venue viability, not as a forecast of future Muevelo operations.

The Muevelo Difference

Same Venue. Different Operator.

Historical Chico's performance is important in establishing venue value and operating history. However, Muevelo does not intend to operate the business in the same manner.

Management intends to implement:

  • Active management oversight
  • Consistent marketing campaigns
  • Strategic event programming
  • Community engagement initiatives
  • Influencer partnerships
  • Customer experience improvements

The objective is not to replicate Chico's operations. The objective is to maximize the potential of the venue through more intentional execution.

Transition Strategy & Continuity Plan

A Structured Handoff

Management has negotiated a transition structure designed to reduce operational risk. As part of the acquisition:

  • Existing Chico's ownership is expected to remain involved for approximately 3 months following closing
  • Existing vendor relationships will be transferred
  • Existing operational knowledge will be shared
  • Existing customer awareness will be preserved
  • Existing staff relationships will be maintained where appropriate

This transition period is intended to create continuity while Muevelo implements its operating strategy.

Licenses, Permits & Regulatory Position

Approvals Already In Place

A major benefit of the acquisition is the continuity of existing operational approvals. Management expects to obtain and continue operating under the same licenses currently utilized by Chico's, including:

  • Type 48 On-Sale General Public Premises License
  • Cabaret Permit
  • Entertainment and Dance Permits
  • Existing Business Licenses
  • Other operational approvals required for venue operations

This continuity significantly reduces startup risk and accelerates the transition process.
