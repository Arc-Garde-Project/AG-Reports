How It Runs

Every dollar is tracked from the ad to the chair.

01 Build The offer, the audience, the landing page, and the CRM are built and tested before a dollar of ad spend goes live.
02 Test 2 ads run head to head for 4 days. The loser is cut. A new challenger takes its place. Same budget, repeated.
03 Attribution Every lead is tracked to the specific ad that produced it, through the CRM instead of the ad platform's own reporting.
04 Reporting A written report every 4 days during testing, every 7 days once a winner is holding. Numbers only.
No projections

Every report says what happened, on real spend, in a dated window.We do not model a forecast and we do not put a number on a slide that has not already been paid for.
