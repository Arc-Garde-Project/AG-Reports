02

The System

Five pieces, in this order. The two marked in gold are yours, built in your name.

The Ad
Facebook and Instagram. Video, scripted and filmed.
Landing Page
Custom coded, on your domain, carrying the form and the tags.
The CRM
In your name. Leads land live and every stage is tracked.
Attribution
Each lead tied back to the ad that produced it.
The Report
A written window on real spend, published on a fixed day.

Attribution runs through the CRM, not the ad platform.

Meta restricts conversion tracking on dental accounts, so the platform's own lead reporting cannot be relied on.

We learned that on a live dental account and built the system around it, which is why the CRM sits at the center rather than the pixel.

How It Runs

Every dollar is tracked from the ad to the lead.

2 ads run head to head for 4 days. The loser is cut. A new challenger takes its place. Same budget, repeated.
03

Leads are tracked to the ad that produced them, through the CRM instead of the ad platform's own reporting.
