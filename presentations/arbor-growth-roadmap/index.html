The Roadmap

Six phases, start to launch

Where each phase begins, and roughly when.
Full detail on the following slides.
Phase 0

Accounts & Tooling

Week 1–2
  • New Facebook Business Page under Frank@arborwealthstrategies.com.
    The old page is tied to his New York Life days and can't be recovered or reused
  • Connect that page to Proofpoint before any finance content runs on it.
    Hard precondition, not a nice-to-have
  • GoHighLevel account creation and setup.
    Greenlit, build the lead routing here
  • Start A2P/10DLC verification for SMS early.
    Carrier-side queues are easy to underestimate
  • Stand up the master lead-tracking sheet
Phase 1

Research & Strategy

Week 1–3 · runs parallel to Phase 0
  • Market, outlier and avatar research
  • Angle ideation
  • Copywriting framework
  • Offer crystallization.
    Keep it "complimentary consultation," never "free"
  • Three dedicated funnels built: business, medical, athlete
Phase 2

Lead Sourcing

Week 2–4
  • Scrape 1,500 leads: 500 business owners, 500 medical professionals, 500 professional athletes
  • Leads load into the master tracking sheet
Note

Qualifiers for the 1,500 leads are still "to be determined by Arbor Wealth."
This phase can't fully start, or the list can't be trusted, until Frank or Bryan defines them.

Phase 3

Creative Production: Round 1

Week 3–7+
  • Script writing + description writing: 14 videos, 14 static creatives
  • Monday internal review with Bryan, then WestPac / Guardian compliance
  • Filming begins only after that greenlight lands
  • Editing, then revision rounds
Note

Compliance turnaround runs up to two weeks and is currently slower on staffing.
Budget 2–3 weeks per batch, and don't schedule filming dates until a batch is actually cleared.

Phase 4

Outbound Execution

Ongoing, once Phase 2 qualifiers land
  • 10 connection requests per day, sent one by one from Frank's account
  • Accepted connections messaged Monday through Friday
Note

At 10 requests a day, reaching all 1,500 leads once takes roughly 30 weeks, about seven months, before any follow-up messaging.
Worth confirming that pace is deliberate rather than a resourcing gap.

Phase 5

Paid Launch

Week 6–8+
  • Ad concepts to compliance for pre-review before any spend
  • Media buying begins once the page, Proofpoint and creative are all live
  • 4-day A/B sprints, run back to back
