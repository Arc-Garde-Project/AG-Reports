Investor Participation Disclaimer

Ownership of shares in the Founding Investor Circle does not grant voting rights, management authority, or any decision-making power over the operations of Muevelo West. All investors in the Founding Investor Circle are silent partners with no controlling interest. Day-to-day operations, strategic direction, staffing, programming, and all business decisions remain solely under the authority of the founding operators (Mario & Henry Trujillo, 51%).

Investor distributions will not commence until the seller carry note ($100,000, 12-month term) has been repaid in full. Repayment of this note will be prioritized from early operating profits, with accelerated paydown when possible. Following full retirement of the seller carry obligation, investor distributions will be made proportionally based on each investor’s share ownership within the 49% Founding Investor Circle pool.

Executive Summary

Muevelo West combines the established Muevelo nightlife brand with the long-standing Chico’s Montebello venue, creating a concept that launches with an existing audience and recognizable location.

The investment structure allocates 51% ownership to the founding operators and 49% to the Founding Investor Circle, providing investors the opportunity to participate in the early growth of the brand.

Projected attendance ranges from 110 to 180 guests per night with an estimated average spend of ~$50 per guest.

Under the base scenario, projected monthly revenue is ~$97,500 with upside potential through special programming, themed events, and expanded operating concepts.

The business is structured to prioritize stable operations and cash flow in the early stages while creating long‑term brand equity and potential enterprise value growth.

Investment Opportunity

Muevelo West represents the relocation of the successful Muevelo nightlife brand into the historic Chico’s Montebello venue. Muevelo already brings an established audience and event demand, while Chico’s Montebello provides a recognizable nightlife location.

This combination allows the concept to launch with built‑in awareness and significantly lower risk than a traditional nightclub startup.

The total capital requirement for the project is approximately $720,000 including acquisition, renovations, rebranding, legal, inventory, and working capital.

Acquisition Structure:

$600,000 purchase price structured as $500,000 cash at close + $100,000 seller carry (12-month term). Early Year 1 profits will be directed toward paying down the seller carry ahead of schedule.

Venue and Lease Control

The venue operates under a five‑year lease with three additional five‑year extensions. If all options are exercised, operators could control the location for up to twenty years.

This long‑term control allows the ownership group to build brand equity, grow a loyal audience, and stabilize operations in the market.

Additional transition provisions include: a minimum of 1 year for use of the Chico name on exterior signage, and a minimum of 3 months of access to Chico’s existing social media platforms to support audience migration and brand continuity.

Rebranding Strategy

Phase 1: Year 1

Operate Under Chico’s Name

  • Retain “Chico’s Montebello” branding on exterior signage for a minimum of 12 months per agreement
  • Leverage Chico’s social media (min. 3 months access) to migrate audience to Muevelo channels
  • Softly introduce Muevelo brand elements — interior decor, events, merch, and digital presence
  • Stabilize operations, build customer trust, and pay down seller carry note
  • Reduced Year 1 profit expectations due to transition costs and seller carry obligations

Phase 2: Year 2+

Full Muevelo Transition

  • Complete exterior signage update to Muevelo West brand identity
  • Full decor refresh, branded merchandise, and Muevelo-first event calendar
  • Seller carry fully repaid; cash flow stabilizes for investor distributions
  • Full Muevelo social and digital presence; expanded programming and partnerships
  • Year 2 positioned as growth phase — stabilized profitability and brand equity building

Revenue Growth Opportunities

Muevelo West’s base projections intentionally assume a conservative operating model focused on core weekend traffic and beverage sales. However, nightlife venues often grow revenue significantly through special programming, branded events, and community engagement. These initiatives can increase guest counts, average spend per visitor, and overall brand equity.

Theme Nights (Latin Night, Throwback Nights, Pride Nights, etc.) that drive repeat attendance and niche audiences.

Guest DJs, entertainers, or drag performers that attract new crowds and allow ticketed or premium cover pricing.

Influencer or celebrity host nights that create social buzz and increase organic marketing reach.

Corporate events, birthday celebrations, and community gatherings that can be booked during off‑hours or early evenings.

Sunday Funday concepts such as Beer Bust or Tardeada-style afternoon parties that can expand operating hours and daytime revenue.

Brand sponsorships and beverage promotions that can generate additional marketing support and revenue partnerships.
