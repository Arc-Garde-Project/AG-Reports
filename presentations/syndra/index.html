Deliverables & Services

Everything Included

01

Infrastructure Setup

Complete cold email infrastructure capable of sending 10,000 emails per day. Includes domain acquisition and configuration, DNS setup (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), inbox warming across all sending accounts, and deliverability optimization to ensure consistent inbox placement.

02

Lead List Building

Ongoing prospecting and list development tailored to target market criteria. Includes data sourcing, verification, enrichment, and segmentation. Lists delivered on a rolling basis throughout the 90-day engagement.

03

Copywriting

Development of cold email sequences including initial outreach, follow-ups, and re-engagement campaigns. Copy will be tested and iterated based on performance data. Includes A/B testing of subject lines, hooks, and CTAs.

04

CRM Automation

Integration with client's existing CRM or setup of a new system. Automated tagging, lead scoring, and pipeline workflows to ensure no opportunities fall through the cracks. Real-time sync between email system and CRM.

05

Inbox Management

Daily monitoring and management of all sending inboxes. Includes reply sorting, positive response flagging, bounce handling, and unsubscribe management. Spam complaint mitigation and sender reputation monitoring.

06

Lead Qualification & Handoff

Full qualification of all positive replies including back-and-forth communication to confirm fit and intent. Only sales-ready, qualified leads are handed off to client via preferred method (CRM, Slack, email, or spreadsheet).

Days 30–90

Day 90

Review & Continuation

Performance review and discussion of continuation terms. Options include monthly retainer or revenue share model based on campaign success.
