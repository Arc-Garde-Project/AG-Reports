Referral Engine

Customers Bring Customers.

Every customer gets a unique referral link. They text it to friends. When the friend books and pays, both earn bonus points. Built-in fraud protection catches gaming.

Anti-fraud built in: Self-referral blocking, duplicate detection, phone validation, velocity limits, staff cross-checks. Every suspicious pattern flagged for your review.
Payment Logging

Two Ways to Trigger Points.

Stripe automates everything. Manual entry handles cash and checks. Both are equally reliable.

⚡

Stripe (Automatic)

Customer pays an invoice. Points are awarded instantly. Zero manual work. Add anytime.

✍️

Manual Entry (15 sec)

Cash or check? Enter customer, amount, type. Points held 24hrs for fraud protection. Full audit trail.

Communications

Reach Them by Text and Email.

Automated texts for instant notifications. Email newsletters for promotions. Both channels built in.

📲

SMS via Twilio

📧

Email via Resend

Balance reminders, seasonal promos, referral nudges. Audience filtering. Free under 3,000/mo.
