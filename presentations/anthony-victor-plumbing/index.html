The Solution

THREE ENGINES. ONE PLATFORM.

A custom-built web app that turns every completed job into repeat business, referrals, and rewards -- without you lifting a finger.

01
REPEAT BUSINESS ENGINE

Every dollar a customer spends earns them 1 point automatically. A $400 water heater flush earns 400 points -- worth $20 toward their next service. The system knows what was serviced and when. 11 months later? Automatic reminder: "Your annual flush is due -- and you have rewards waiting." One-time callers become 2-3x per year customers.

02
REFERRAL TRACKING ENGINE

After every job, your customer gets a personal referral link via text. No app download needed. They forward it to a neighbor. When that neighbor books and completes their first paid job, the referrer earns 500 bonus points ($25 value) and the new customer gets 200 points ($10 off). You see exactly who your top referrers are, how much revenue each one has generated, and the full referral chain.

03
REWARDS REDEMPTION ENGINE

Points redeem for credits on future services, gift cards, or discounts through partner businesses. Your customers choose how they want to use their rewards. 400 points gets them $20 off their next job. Or they can save up for bigger rewards. Every redemption is tracked, and every dollar back drives the next booking.

How It Works

THE CUSTOMER JOURNEY

From first call to loyal customer -- zero extra work for you or your team.

Step 1
CUSTOMER BOOKS A JOB

Customer calls or books online. Your team handles the job like normal. Nothing changes about your current workflow.

Step 2
INVOICE IS PAID

When the customer pays (digital or manual entry for cash/check), the system detects the payment automatically. Points are calculated and credited instantly. No scanning, no check-ins, no extra steps.

Step 3
CUSTOMER GETS A TEXT

"You just earned 400 points with Anthony Plumbing! That's $20 toward your next service. See your balance: [link]." Sent automatically 2 hours after payment -- when satisfaction is highest.

Step 4
REFERRAL LINK DELIVERED

Along with their points notification, the customer gets their unique referral link. "Know someone who needs a plumber? When they book and pay, they get $10 off and you earn $25 in rewards." They forward the text -- no app needed for either party.

Step 5
THE CYCLE REPEATS

The referred neighbor books a $350 drain cleaning, gets $10 off, and earns 350 points of their own. Now they get a referral link too. Your original customer has 900 points -- $45 toward their next service. One job became three.

Result
A CUSTOMER FOR LIFE

One emergency call becomes a maintenance customer. That customer refers two neighbors. Your business grows without a single ad dollar spent.
