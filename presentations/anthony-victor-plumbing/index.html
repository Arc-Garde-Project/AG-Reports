The Problem

ONE JOB. THEN GONE.

Most plumbing businesses lose customers after a single service call. Not because the work was bad, but because there is no system to bring them back.

NO REPEAT SYSTEM

Customer calls, you fix the problem, they pay. Then nothing. No follow-up. No reason to call you again until something else breaks -- which could be months or years.

REFERRALS ARE INVISIBLE

Your best customers refer their neighbors, friends, coworkers. But you have no idea who referred who. No way to track it. No way to reward it. No way to encourage more of it.

REVIEWS DON'T HAPPEN

Happy customers forget to leave reviews. You are too busy running jobs to chase them. Meanwhile, competitors with more reviews win the Google search.

The Solution

FIVE ENGINES. ONE PLATFORM.

A custom-built web app that turns every completed job into repeat business, referrals, and reviews -- without you lifting a finger.

01
REPEAT BUSINESS ENGINE

Every dollar a customer spends earns them points toward future discounts. But unlike a coffee shop punch card, this system knows what was serviced and when. 11 months after a water heater install? Automatic reminder: "Your annual flush is due -- and you have $35 in rewards waiting." This turns one-time callers into 2-3x per year customers.

02
REFERRAL TRACKING ENGINE

After every job, your customer gets a personal referral link via text. No app download needed. They forward it to a neighbor, the neighbor books, both get credit automatically. You see exactly who your top referrers are, how much business each one has generated, and the full referral chain -- every handshake tracked.

03
GOOGLE REVIEW ENGINE

Two hours after a job is paid, your customer gets a text: "Thanks for choosing A&V Plumbing! Leave a quick review and earn 80 points." One tap takes them straight to your Google review page. No app, no login, no friction. Points credited automatically when they confirm. Strike while satisfaction is highest.

04
SMART OFFERS ENGINE

Automated, targeted offers based on service history -- not generic blasts. Seasonal reminders (winterize pipes, summer irrigation check). Equipment lifecycle alerts (your garbage disposal is 8 years old -- time for a checkup). Inactivity re-engagement (haven't called in 12 months? Here is a $25 credit). Each one drives a booking without you thinking about it.

05
BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE DASHBOARD

See everything in one place. Revenue per customer, referral attribution, Google review velocity, maintenance plan conversion rate, seasonal demand patterns. Not just a loyalty tracker -- a full picture of your customer base and what it is worth.

How It Works

THE CUSTOMER JOURNEY

From first call to loyal customer -- zero extra work for you or your team.

Step 1
CUSTOMER BOOKS A JOB

Customer calls or books online. Your team handles the job like normal. Nothing changes about your current workflow.

Step 2
INVOICE IS PAID

When the customer pays their invoice (Stripe, Square, or manual entry for cash/check), the system detects the payment automatically. Points are calculated and credited instantly. No scanning, no check-ins, no extra steps.

Step 3
CUSTOMER GETS A TEXT

"You just earned 450 points with A&V Plumbing! You're $50 away from a $25 reward. See your balance: [link]." Plus a Google review request with bonus points. All automatic, timed for 2 hours after payment when satisfaction is highest.

Step 4
REFERRAL LINK DELIVERED

Along with their points notification, the customer gets their unique referral link. "Know someone who needs a plumber? They get $50 off, you get $50 credit." They can forward the text anytime -- no app download required for either party.

Step 5
SMART REMINDERS KICK IN

Based on what was serviced, the system schedules follow-ups automatically. Water heater flush in 11 months. Drain cleaning in 6. Winterization before October. Each reminder includes their reward balance -- a reason to book now instead of later.

Result
A CUSTOMER FOR LIFE

One emergency call becomes a maintenance customer. That customer refers two neighbors. Those neighbors leave Google reviews. Your business grows without a single ad dollar spent.

Before We Build

QUESTIONS FOR YOU

To build this right, we need to understand a few things about your business.

1
Residential, commercial, or both?

Residential customers use the loyalty system directly. Commercial contracts (property managers, building owners) need a different approach -- maintenance agreements and vendor portals rather than points and referrals. This changes what we build first.

2
How do you currently collect payment?

Stripe, Square, QuickBooks, cash, check -- each one determines how points get triggered automatically. The more digital your payments, the less manual work.

3
How many active customers do you have?

This tells us the starting database size, expected transaction volume, and how quickly the referral engine can compound. 200 is different from 2,000.

4
What services make up most of your revenue?

We want to weight the loyalty system toward your highest-margin services. If drain cleaning is your bread and butter, that is where maintenance reminders should focus. If big installs drive revenue, referral bonuses should reflect that ticket size.

5
Do you currently have maintenance plans?

If yes, the loyalty system wraps around them and boosts conversion. If no, this platform is the vehicle to launch them -- built-in from day one.

6
How many trucks / technicians?

Determines how many staff accounts we need, whether field techs need a mobile view for manual point entry on cash jobs, and the scale of daily transactions the system needs to handle.

7
Multiple locations now or planned?

Multi-location support needs to be designed into the system from the start -- shared customer points, per-location reporting, staff permissions. Better to plan for it now than rebuild later.

8
What does "success" look like to you in year one?

More repeat customers? More Google reviews? A specific revenue number from referrals? We build the dashboard and the point structure around the metric that matters most to you.
