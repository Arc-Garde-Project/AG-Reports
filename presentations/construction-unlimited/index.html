How it runs

No surprises. 3 weeks, 3 stages, 2 rounds to get it right.

Same order, every time. Nothing goes on until what is under it is right.

01

Week 1, structure

Structure gets locked first. Every page gets mapped against the offer and the quote flow: what a homeowner needs to see, and in what order, to pick up the phone or fill out the form. Copy and structure are approved before anything gets designed.

02

Week 2, build

Pages get built. 5 pages coded, the quote form wired to route to your team, call tracking installed and tested, mobile checked on real phones.

03

Week 3, revisions and launch

2 rounds of revisions: word changes, image swaps, layout adjustments inside the built structure. Once approved, the site goes live, DNS is pointed, and every tracked piece is confirmed working before we call it done.

Reporting

A short update at the end of each week: what's built, what's next, and a live link to review it yourself. No dashboard login required.

What we need

Access and material required to start.

  • Domain registrar access or DNS login
  • Current hosting details, if the site already lives somewhere
  • Logo and any existing brand assets
  • Photos of finished roofs and crew, or approval to use stock until your own are ready
  • Licenses, certifications, or reviews you want featured
  • The number and the financing offer the site and ads will run on
  • CRM login or API access, if you already run one, so the quote form can route into it

CRM is your cost, not part of this build. GoHighLevel is recommended if you don't already have one, but the build works with any established CRM.
