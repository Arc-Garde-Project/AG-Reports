How it runs

No Surprises. 3 Weeks, 3 Stages.

Same order, every time. Nothing goes on until what is under it is right.

01

Week 1, Structure

Structure gets locked first. Every page gets mapped against the offer and the quote flow: what a homeowner needs to see, and in what order, to pick up the phone or fill out the form. Copy and structure are approved before anything gets designed.

02

Week 2, Build

Pages get built. 5 pages coded, the quote form wired to route to your team, form submissions tracked and reported, mobile checked on real phones.

03

Week 3, Revisions and Launch

2 rounds of revisions: word changes, image swaps, layout adjustments inside the built structure. Once approved, the site goes live, DNS is pointed, and every tracked piece is confirmed working before we call it done.
