The approach

Enterprise buyers don't fall through funnels

For a high-ticket, technical sale, ads don't convert the room. Presence and precision do.

01

They check you first

Before a decision-maker replies, they open your profile. A sparse account ends the conversation before it starts, so we build a credible presence first.

02

Then we snipe

7 to 12 targeted LinkedIn connections a day to the exact decision-makers, opened with a real conversation, not a pitch. Skip-tracing finds the right person and department.

03

Ads come later

These buyers move through relationships and RFPs, not ad funnels. Paid only makes sense once you add a lower-ticket tier anyone can buy.
