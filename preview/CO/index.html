The Problem

You've been thinking about selling.

Maybe you checked Zillow.

Maybe a friend gave you a number.

Maybe you are navigating something more complex:

  • a divorce
  • an estate
  • a trust
  • a rental property that is underperforming
  • or a foreclosure notice that showed up in the mail.

Whatever brought you here, the situation is the same.

You are about to make a decision involving hundreds of thousands of dollars, and nobody has answered the three questions that actually matter.

Question 1

What is your home really worth based on actual comparable sales, not an algorithm?

Is now even the right time to sell based on your specific financial situation?
